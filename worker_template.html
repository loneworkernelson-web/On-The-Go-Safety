Safety & Duress PINs

Standard Safety PIN: This code is used exclusively to declare yourself safe after an alarm has been activated. Entering this PIN is the only way to confirm to the system that you are secure and to stop the alert from escalating.

Silent Duress PIN: This is used to silently declare yourself unsafe when you are required to clear an alarm under threat. The app will appear to clear the alarm normally, but it will immediately and silently notify your contacts that you require help.

Nothing on this page sends an alarm or tells anyone. Take your time.

1. The SOS button

During a visit the red SOS button is on your screen. Tap it three times quickly - each tap within 2 seconds of the last.

Tap 3 times.

What happens next in a real alarm:

- Your screen turns slightly amber with a grainy look. No siren and no flashing, so nobody nearby can tell.

- Your contacts and monitor are alerted straight away, with your location.

- To stand it down, hold "I'm safe" and enter your safety PIN.

- If someone makes you stand it down, enter your duress PIN instead. Your screen looks the same, but your monitor is told you are under threat.

2. Your PINs

Type a PIN to check which one it is. It is checked on this phone only - nothing is saved or sent.

3. The shake alert

Choose a level, then test it. Standard suits most people. The easier levels need a gentler shake, but are easier to set off by accident.
